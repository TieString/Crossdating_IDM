Reconstruction policy

- No source slide is inserted as a full-page image or background.
- Titles, labels, numbers, annotations, axes, plots, connectors, cards, and flow elements are rebuilt as editable PowerPoint objects.
- Source PNG crops are used only for complex tree-core texture and watercolor background decoration.
- All cropped-image objects remain independent and selectable.
